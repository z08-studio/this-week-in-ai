# This Week in AI

![This Week in AI — a weekly newsletter about AI models, tools, workflows, and production delivery](assets/banner.png)

**This Week in AI** is a weekly newsletter published every Monday, dedicated to tracking developments in artificial intelligence and how people put them to work.

It covers models and APIs, tools and agents, research, creative workflows, and lessons from building and delivering AI products.

[Subscribe on Substack](https://thatweekinai.substack.com) to receive the latest issue in your inbox.

## Suggest content

Have something useful to share with AI readers?

[Submit an issue](https://github.com/z08-studio/this-week-in-ai/issues/new?template=content-suggestion.yml) to suggest a model, tool, research paper, or practical workflow for a future edition.
